How it works

From a family list to a chart on your wall

  1. Send your family list

    Fill in our paper form, or a simple spreadsheet: names, who is whose child, who married whom. Birth dates and mobile numbers are optional.

  2. We draw the chart

    Each generation becomes a ring around the eldest couple. The chart sizes itself so every branch fits, however large the family.

  3. Your family adds photos

    You get a private link to share in the family group. Relatives add a photo for each person from their phone, and fill in missing birth dates and mobile numbers. We check every photo first.

  4. Receive your chart

    You get a sharp, print-ready PDF, plus an image to share on WhatsApp. Print it at any shop, at any size.

Questions

Good to know

How large a family can you fit?

Hundreds of people. The chart works out its own size so every branch fits. For very large families we suggest a bigger sheet, such as A1 or A0, or separate charts for each branch so names stay easy to read.

What does it cost?

It depends mostly on the size of the family and how many branch charts you would like. Send an enquiry with a rough number of people and we will send you a quote.

Do we have to give photos for everyone?

No. Anyone without a photo appears with their initials in a coloured circle: blue for men, pink for women. You can add photos later and we will update the chart.

Can names be in Hindi or another script?

Yes. Tell us when you enquire, and we will confirm how your chart will look before you order.

Who can see our family's details?

Only us, and the family members you share your private photo link with. That link shows the names, birth dates and mobile numbers on your list so relatives can fill in and correct them, so please share it only within the family. It never shows the photos others have sent, and it stops working when you ask or when it expires. Read our privacy notice for the details.

Do you print the chart?

We deliver a print-ready PDF that any print shop can print, at the size you choose. Ask us if you would like help with printing.
