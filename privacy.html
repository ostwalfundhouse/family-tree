Privacy notice

Last updated: date

This notice explains what personal data we collect to make your family tree chart, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. "We" means business name and address.

What we collect and why

  • Enquiry details (your name, mobile, email, city, rough family size, your message). We use these to reply to you and send a quote.
  • Your family list (names, who is related to whom, and optionally birth dates and mobile numbers). We use this only to draw your chart.
  • Family photos that you or your relatives upload through your private link. We use these only to place photos on your chart.
  • Changes made through your private link to birth dates and mobile numbers, with the earlier value and the time of the change, so that mistakes can be undone, and suggestions sent through it (name corrections, new family members, removals) until we have reviewed them.
  • Basic technical records that our hosting providers keep to run the service securely, such as the time a link was last opened.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use your family's details or photos for anything other than your chart.

Consent

We process your enquiry details because you agree to it when you send the form. Family details and photos are processed with the consent of the person who orders the chart, who agrees to our terms and shares the private link within the family.

Lawyer to confirm: how consent is handled for relatives who are not the customer, and for children under 18 (the Act requires verifiable parental consent).

You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us (see "Contact" below). We will then stop using the data and delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Who can see it

  • Only we can see your full family list and all photos.
  • Anyone with your private photo link can see the names on your list, relation hints such as "son of", and each person's birth date and mobile number, and can add or change those details. They can also suggest name corrections, new family members or removals, which we check before using. They cannot see the photos others have sent. Please share the link only within the family. You can ask us to switch the link off at any time, and every link has an expiry date.
  • Anyone who scans the QR code of your chart, or has its link, can view the chart on screen, without a download or share button. If you ask for a password, only people who know it can. Mobile numbers appear only if you asked for them to be on the chart.
  • Our service providers store data for us under contract: Supabase (database and file storage, Mumbai region, India) and Vercel (website hosting).

How long we keep it

  • Family lists and photos: deleted 12 months after we deliver your chart, or sooner if you ask. If a chart is never finished, they are deleted after 24 months without any changes.
  • Photos we reject or that are replaced: deleted within 30 days.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to an order: deleted after 24 months.
  • Photo links: deleted 30 days after they expire.
  • Charts with a QR code for framing, and permanent family links: so that a framed QR code keeps working, we keep the family list and photos behind it for as long as that QR code or link is switched on. Ask us to switch it off, and the deletion periods above apply again.

An automatic clean-up runs every night to carry out these deletions.

Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or complete it;
  • delete it;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to.

We will reply within number days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored in private storage that only we can open. Photo links use long random codes that cannot be guessed.
