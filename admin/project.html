          Final chart (PDF) for the customer

          Makes the print-ready PDF from the chart preview below, using the preview's Options (birth dates, mobile numbers, title), and a QR code to forward or frame. The QR never changes: scanning it opens this PDF on the phone, view only, with no download or share button. After family changes, press Update PDF and the same QR shows the new version. If the QR will be framed, switch mobile numbers off in the preview's Options first.
